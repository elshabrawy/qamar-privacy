🌕 Qamar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Shebo Tech

This policy explains what information the Qamar mobile game ("the game", "we") collects. Contact: mohamed.saeed.elshabrawy@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

The current version of Qamar is played offline against a computer opponent. It does not collect, send or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your settings (language, sound on/off and color theme) are stored only on your device and never leave it.

2. Future online features

If a future update adds online matches, friends or ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, describing exactly what is collected and why.

3. Children

Qamar is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

4. Your rights

You can contact us at any time at the email above with questions about your privacy.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
